# Wheat Raja Research and Design Direction

## Competitor Patterns Studied

- Aashirvaad, Fortune and Pillsbury lean heavily on brand trust, family cooking, nutrition claims and familiar atta category cues.
- Organic Tattva and premium organic D2C references use cleaner grids, ingredient transparency, badges, subscriptions and stronger product education.
- Fresh chakki brands such as Chakkizza, Urban Chakki and Anmasa emphasize milling freshness, local delivery, purity and softer rotis.
- Premium global flour and organic food ecommerce sites tend to use cinematic crop imagery, restrained copy, generous spacing, simple bundles and trust-first checkout.

## Design System

- Palette: wheat gold, forest green, warm cream, mitti brown, clay accent and clean white.
- Typography: editorial serif for emotional brand moments, Inter-style sans for commerce clarity.
- Components: fixed rounded cards at 8px, pill CTAs, trust badges, dense admin cards, image-led product cards.
- Motion: quiet page transitions, product hover scale, subtle parallax-ready full-bleed video hero.

## Conversion Ideas Applied

- Hero promise is short and purchase-oriented: Fresh Atta, Milled After Your Order.
- Trust appears before product browsing: freshly milled, stone-ground, zero preservatives, delivered in Noida.
- PDPs include weight selection, subscribe and save, badges, delivery checker, FAQs and schema.
- Checkout models OTP login, saved address, coupon, wallet and Razorpay handoff.
- Admin IA covers delivery radius, wallet controls, referrals, reviews moderation and CMS.
